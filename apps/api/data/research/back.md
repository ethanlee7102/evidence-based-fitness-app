# Back Exercises - EMG-Backed Muscle Activation Research

Status: 33/33 exercises researched. All mappings verified against EMG studies from ACE (Edelburg/Porcari 2018), Fenwick et al. 2009 (JSCR), Youdas et al. 2010, Dickie et al. 2017, Lusk et al. 2010, Signorile et al. 2002 (PMC449729), Botton et al. 2013, and related PMC/PubMed literature.

Activation levels: maximum (primary mover), high (major synergist), medium (contributes but not primary), partial (stabilizer/minimal)

*Sources: ACE-sponsored research (Edelburg/Porcari 2018) — bent-over row activated 3 of 5 back muscles to greatest degree; highest erector spinae activation of all back exercises tested. Fenwick et al. 2009 (JSCR) — large symmetric activation from upper to lower back, highest lumbar spine load.*

## Cross-Exercise Consistency Notes

### Lat Activation Hierarchy (based on ACE 2018 + Youdas 2010)
- **Pull-Up / Chin-Up**: highest lat activation (117-130% MVIC) → maximum
- **Lat Pulldown / Cable Lat Pulldown**: high lat activation, below pull-ups → maximum (bodyweight disadvantage explains MVIC difference, not actual motor unit recruitment)
- **Barbell Row / Dumbbell Row**: high lat activation → maximum
- **Seated Cable Row**: good lat activation → maximum (upper), high (lower)
- **Inverted Row**: moderate lat activation, below pull-ups → high
- **Assisted Pull-Up**: reduced load = reduced absolute activation → high

### Biceps Activation: Supinated > Neutral > Pronated
- **Chin-Up / Reverse-Grip Lat Pulldown**: supinated → biceps high-to-maximum
- **Neutral-Grip Pull-Up**: neutral → biceps high
- **Pull-Up / Wide-Grip Lat Pulldown**: pronated → biceps high (pull-up) or medium (wide-grip)

### Erector Spinae: Free-standing > Seated > Chest-supported
- **Barbell Row / T-Bar Row**: standing bent-over → high
- **Pendlay Row**: dead-stop reduces sustained isometric hold → medium
- **Seated Cable Row**: seated with back support → partial
- **Chest-Supported / Seal Row**: chest pad eliminates need → not listed (negligible)

### Rear Delt Isolation Hierarchy
- **Face Pull / Reverse Pec Deck**: 80-90% MVIC → maximum
- **Rows (various)**: meaningful contribution but not primary → high
- **Pull-Ups/Pulldowns**: minor contributor → medium to partial

### Unilateral Anti-Rotation Effect
- All single-arm exercises (Dumbbell Row, Meadows Row, Single-Arm Cable Row, Landmine Row, Renegade Row) include oblique activation at medium level due to 59-63% greater oblique demand vs bilateral (Saeterbakken et al. 2015)
