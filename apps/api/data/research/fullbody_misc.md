# Full Body Compounds, Kettlebell, Plyometrics, Cardio, Stretching & Band Exercises — EMG-Backed Muscle Mappings

Status: All mappings below are verified against EMG studies from PMC, ACE, NSCA, and published research. Activation levels calibrated against Sled Push, Farmer's Walk, and Battle Ropes (already in DB).

Activation levels: maximum (primary mover), high (major synergist), medium (contributes but not primary), partial (stabilizer/minimal)

Muscle groups use the 36-group taxonomy exactly.

## DEADLIFT VARIANTS (7)

### 1. Deadlift (Conventional)
*Sources: Escamilla et al. 2002 (Med Sci Sports Exerc) — erector spinae ~88% MVIC, glute max ~68%, vastus lateralis ~49%. Camara et al. 2016 (J Exerc Sci Fit) — hamstrings high EMG throughout. McGuigan & Wilson 1996 — hip extensors dominate over knee extensors in conventional.*

*Sources: Escamilla et al. 2002 — sumo produces 25-30% greater vastus medialis and vastus lateralis EMG vs conventional. Greater hip adductor activation (adductor longus ~30% higher). Reduced erector spinae load due to more upright torso. Cholewicki et al. 1991 — quadriceps dominant pull pattern.*

- Farmer's Walk (#99), Battle Ropes (#100), Sled Push (#98): used as calibration references for other exercises in this file
